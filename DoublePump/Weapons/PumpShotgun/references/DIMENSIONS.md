# Dimension and proportion targets

Units: metres in Blender (1 BU = 1 m). Muzzle points **+Y**, right side **+X**, up **+Z**.

Legend: **C** = confirmed from a cited source; **E** = estimate (reasoned from
photos/typical 12 ga parts or from the stylised Fortnite look); **S** = deliberate
stylisation for Roblox readability.

## Roblox scale
Roblox treats **1 stud = 0.28 m** for physics/character scale. Import the
exports with Studio's 3D Importer **scale unit = Meters**; the 1.02 m gun then
becomes ≈ 3.6 studs, next to a ≈ 5.5-stud R15 character (≈ 1.55 m equivalent).
See README "Scale" for the full conversion.
