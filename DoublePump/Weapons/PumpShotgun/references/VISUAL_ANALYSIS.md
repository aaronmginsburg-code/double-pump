# Visual analysis — 2017–2018 Fortnite pump shotgun

Legend: **[source]** = stated in a cited text source (see REFERENCES.md);
**[recollection]** = author's memory of the in-game model, not verified against
an image in this environment (image hosts were blocked).

## Identity
- Lower-rarity model (grey/green/blue) is an 870-style pump: **[source F3]**
  "based on the Remington 870, with rifle sights". **[source F1]** also calls the
  older model trench-gun inspired and mentions a sight/laser on top of the barrel.
- Higher rarities (purple/gold) used a different, SPAS-12-like model **[source F3]**.
  Double Pump uses only the 870-style look.

## Silhouette  [recollection]
- Long, straight, horizontal line from muzzle to receiver; the gun reads as
  "long tube + chunky box + long wooden stock".
- Receiver is visibly **oversized** vs a real 870: tall flat sides, rounded top,
  noticeably larger than the wrist of the stock, so it dominates the
  first-person view.
- Barrel and magazine tube are two **clearly separate tubes** stacked
  vertically, joined by a **barrel band** near the muzzle. The tube ends a little
  short of the muzzle.
- **Pump (forend)** is chunky and deeply **grooved** with wide horizontal ribs,
  warm orange-brown wood, sitting well forward of the receiver.
- **Stock** is a full-length wooden stock with a modest drop, straight comb,
  and a dark **recoil pad**; wrist/grip is a slim straight-grip curve, not a
  separate pistol grip.
- **Front sight** blade/post near the muzzle and a small rear sight on the
  receiver top (rifle sights) **[source F3]**.

## Materials  [recollection]
- Metal: dark gunmetal / blued steel, low saturation, with soft painted-style
  highlights and gentle edge lightening. Not chrome, not matte black plastic.
- Wood: saturated **warm orange-brown** (more orange than real walnut), visible
  long grain, light edge wear.
- Overall stylisation: clean, readable, slightly cartoon-exaggerated
  proportions, big bevels, few tiny details.

## What makes it read well in first person  [design conclusions]
1. The **top of the receiver and the barrel line** are what the player sees most:
   a clean rounded receiver top, a visible rear sight notch and a tall front
   post make the gun read as "aimed down the barrel".
2. The **pump** is in the lower-right of frame and moves every shot; its grooves
   and the action bars sliding into the receiver sell the mechanics.
3. The **ejection port** is on the right side (as on a real 870), so a right-handed
   first-person camera mostly sees the gun's **left** side and top. The left side
   therefore needs its own interest (relief panel, pins, seams), and the spent
   shell arcing up and right out of the port is still visible during the pump.
4. Warm wood vs cool dark steel gives instant contrast at Roblox resolutions.

## What Double Pump changes (originality)
To keep the design original rather than a copy:
- Receiver has its own profile: a raised, serrated **sight rib** along the top,
  milled relief panels on both flat sides, panel-line seams, a bevelled front
  shoulder and a squared rifle-style rear notch block.
- Barrel band is a **two-piece clamp with a hex boss**, not a plain ring.
- Forend uses **3 + 4 deep rings around a smooth central hand band**, with a
  steel rear ferrule that carries the action bars, and a saddle that cups the barrel.
- Stock has a **sculpted wrist**, a ribbed rubber pad with two screws, and a
  sling stud; the magazine cap has grip rings and a sling eye.
- No Epic logos, markings, decals or rarity colouring.
